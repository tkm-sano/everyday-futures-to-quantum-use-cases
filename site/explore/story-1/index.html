Traffic and Energy Coordination in a Smart City During Extreme Heat

Read the story, explore each scene, and discuss what kind of future you would like to see.

Explore the Story

Moment 1

Taro, a university student, was looking for drinking water near a station in Tokyo’s waterfront district. He was about to head to his part-time delivery job, but the convenience store shelves were empty and the lights inside had been dimmed.

Just then, a delivery request appeared on his phone. He remembered that he had registered this time slot as available. Taro relied on delivery work to help cover his living expenses. After glancing once more at the empty shelves, he tapped “Accept Request.”

Moment 2

When Taro arrived at the delivery hub and boarded his assigned vehicle, the destinations and route appeared on the onboard display. His “Route Compliance Score” appeared beside them. Taro felt relieved to see that it met the required standard.

He checked the vehicle’s remaining battery level and the restricted operating zones, consented to the use of his driving data, and set off along the designated route.

Moment 3

Taro drove along the designated route, checking each time to make sure that no violations had been recorded.

During the delivery, he became intensely thirsty. When he selected a nearby water station on the terminal, a warning appeared stating that leaving the designated route would reduce his pay and lower his Route Compliance Score.

Even so, when Taro left the route, the screen displayed the message, “Your driving data may be shared with external evaluation agencies.” Despite the urgency of the situation, Taro recorded both the warning shown on his watch and the notification sent to his smartphone.

Moment 4

Taro is considering raising an objection with the people responsible for the Urban Integrated Management System. Although greater stability in daily life is one of the system’s benefits, he is deeply dissatisfied with a system that does not adequately consider drivers’ health conditions or individual circumstances.
