Smart City

Read the story, explore each scene, and discuss what kind of future you would like to see.

How to Explore the Story

Discuss in your group

  1. Read the moments and choose something that catches your attention.
  2. Describe your reaction.

    Are you curious about it, concerned about it, or interested in improving it?

  3. Share why it caught your attention.

    What change in everyday life do you notice? What question or “what if” does it suggest?

These are not strict categories. They are simply starting points for expressing your reaction to the story. There is no single correct interpretation.

If your group is ready, consider the following

  1. What kind of product, service, system, rule, or social change might make this future better?
  2. Where might quantum technologies contribute—and what other technologies or conditions would also be needed?

We will explore Steps 4 and 5 further in the plenary discussion.

Step 1

Explore the Story

Moment 1

Taro, a university student, was looking for drinking water near a station in the Tokyo Bay area on his way to his part-time delivery job. However, the convenience store shelves were empty and the lights were dimmed. Then a delivery request arrived. Taro, who relied on delivery work to help cover his living expenses, looked at the empty shelves and tapped “Accept Request.”

Moment 2

At the delivery hub, Taro got into his assigned vehicle. The terminal displayed the delivery destination, route, and driver rating. After checking the remaining battery level and restricted areas, he agreed to the use of his operational data and set off.

Moment 3

During the delivery, Taro became extremely thirsty. When he selected a nearby water station, a warning appeared stating that leaving the designated route would reduce his compensation and lower his driver rating.

Moment 4

When Taro left the designated route, the screen displayed: “Your driver rating may be shared with external evaluation organizations.” Despite the urgency of the situation, Taro recorded both the warning shown on his smartwatch and the notification sent to his smartphone.
